### The mechanism: products of confined rules with cubes (closure under multiplication)

**Claim A (proved, trivial).** For any odd m, the set of rules all of whose nonzero eigenvalues at m are cubes is closed under multiplication modulo (x^m − 1, y^m − 1). Hence if f ≡ g·h^3 with g confined at m, f is confined at m. The family is closed under addition, not multiplication, so such products land in the family by accident — which is why the mechanism list (products of *split*, *equal* or *twisted-Frobenius* factors only) misses them.

**Claim B (proved).** Write every family rule as F(u,v) with u = t(x), v = t(y), using T₁=u, T₂=u², T₃=u³+u, T₄=u⁴ (so O(a,b)=T_a(u)T_b(v)+T_b(u)T_a(v), O(a,a)=T_a(u)T_a(v), O(a,0)=T_a(u)+T_a(v), O(0,0)=1). Then eight of the sixteen are g·h³:

**Claim C (proved): the three "g" factors are cubes at 37; (1+u)(1+v) is a cube at 61 while uv and u+v are not.**
- uv = t(x)t(y): cube at 37 because t(ζ^j) = ζ^{−j}(ζ^j+1)² and ζ^j+1 is a cube at 37 (equivalent to the known confinement of rule 90). Not at 61.
- u+v = (xy+1)(x+y)/(xy): eigenvalue ζ^{−j−k}(ζ^{j+k}+1)(ζ^j+ζ^k) = ζ^{−j}(ζ^{j+k}+1)(ζ^{j−k}+1); each factor ζ^n+1 (n≠0) is a Frobenius conjugate of ζ+1, hence a cube at 37; ζ^{−j} is a cube since 3∤m. Not at 61 (same reason as rule 90).
- **Rule-150 lemma.** 1+t(ζ^j) = ζ^{−j}(1+ζ^j+ζ^{2j}) = ζ^{−j}(ζ^{3j}+1)/(ζ^j+1). If 3 ≡ 2^k (mod m) with **k even**, then ζ^{3j}+1 = (ζ^j+1)^{2^k}, so 1+t(ζ^j) = ζ^{−j}(ζ^j+1)^{2^k−1}, and 3 | 2^k−1. So every eigenvalue of 1+t(x) is a cube in F(2^d), hence in F(2^s) (γ∈F(2^s)* is a cube in F(2^s) iff in F(2^d), because (2^d−1)/(2^s−1) = 2^s+1 ≡ 2 mod 3). At m=37: 2^26 ≡ 3, k even. At m=61: 2^6 = 64 ≡ 3, k even. At 61 rule 90 fails (ζ+1 not a cube) but rule 150 succeeds — exactly why 3461, 18246, 26883 are the only ones surviving at 61. Zero eigenvalues: 1+t(ζ^j)=0 would need ζ^j a primitive cube root of unity, impossible.

So the three 61-rules are explained in full, and 1112, 2436, 10498, 13584, 17056 at 37. For 37, the "forced" classes argument also covers the j=0 and diagonal classes automatically, since the identity is a polynomial identity.
