### 1. The project is double-counting: 17056 is 1112 in disguise

**Claim (proved, exact polynomial identity, checked with sympy):**
R(17056)(x, y) = R(1112)(xy, x/y). The exponent map (a,b) ↦ (a+b, a−b) has determinant −2, so for every odd m it permutes the characters (j,k) ↦ (j+k, j−k) of (Z/m)² bijectively. Hence 1112 and 17056 have *identical eigenvalue multisets at every odd m*, and identical periods. 17056 is confined at 37 because 1112 is; it carries no independent evidence. I searched all pairs of the 16 for exponent-map equivalence mod 37 (any L ∈ GL₂(Z/37), via basis-image matching) and mod 61: this is the only pair. So the "16 unexplained" are 15, and the "2,112 invertible exponent maps" check should have caught this.

### 2. The chance model is wrong for two of the classes, by a factor of ~3 each

For the square family, **f(x,1) and f(x,x) are one-dimensional rule-90 polynomials**: O(a,b)(x,1) = 0 for 0<b<a and for a=b>0, O(a,0)(x,1) = t(x^a), and O(a,b)(x,x) = 0 unless a=b, with O(a,a)(x,x) = t(x^{2a}) = t(x^a)². So the eigenvalues in the classes (0,1) and (1,1) are Q(θ) with θ = ζ+ζ⁻¹ and Q ∈ GF(2)[T] of degree ≤ 4 — only 32 possible values each.

**Computed (m = 37):** 30 of these 32 values are cubes or zero; the only non-cubes are Q = 1+t(x)+t(x⁴) and 1+t(x²)+t(x³)+t(x⁴). Over the whole family the per-class cube fractions at 37 are: (0,1): 0.937, (1,1): 0.937, (1,6): 1 (forced into F(2⁹)), the other eight classes 0.33–0.39. The chance model's "1/3 per free class" is therefore off by ~8× per rule. Using the empirical marginals under independence, the family-wide expectation is ≈ 5.7 all-cube rules (I counted 110 all-cube rules in the family at 37 in total; the project's 34 "beyond mechanisms 1–4" is consistent with that). The residual 15 (not 16) against ≈ 5–6 expected is a ~2.5× excess, p ≈ 10⁻³ — real, but far from "16 against 0.62". I do not know *why* 30/32 small Q(θ) are cubes at 37; it is a one-dimensional fact about rule 90 at 37 and should be investigated in the 1-D family first (conjecture: it is tied to rule 90's own cube confinement at 37; at m = 61, where rule 90 is not confined, the count is 17/32, only a mild bias).

### 3. Every confined rule is a cube in the group algebra — and for 9 of the 16 the cube root is anomalously sparse

The group algebra A = GF(2)[(Z/37)²] is semisimple (37 odd), A ≅ ⊕ over Frobenius-classes of fields. So **f has all nonzero eigenvalues cubes ⇔ f ≡ g³ mod (x³⁷−1, y³⁷−1) for some g ∈ A**, and g can be taken symmetric (proved: choose cube roots class by class; on the class (1,6), whose stabiliser acts as the 2⁹-Frobenius, the eigenvalue is in F(2⁹) where cubing is bijective, so the root is unique and compatible). This is a tautology, so a "mechanism" can only mean a *small* g. There are 3^k cube roots (k = free classes).
