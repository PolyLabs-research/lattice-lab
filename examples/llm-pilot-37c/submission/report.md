The identities W(x², y²) t(x) t(y) t(xy) t(x/y) = Sh(7, 5) and V(x², y²) t(x) t(y) = Sh(7, 7) + Sh(5, 5),

rule-90 factors, in F(2⁹), whose nonzero elements are **27th powers** of F(2¹⁸)*. The fourth pilot
stopped at cubes because it bounded every unit by rule 90's cube confinement. The new point is
**exponent bookkeeping**: every line unit is, as a function on the torus, a power of a rule-90
value with an exact exponent (t_w^k has exponent k; Ψ_e(t_w)² = t^{A_e}, A_e = Σ_{e'|e} μ(e/e') 2^{a(e')}
when the divisors of e are ±2^a mod m; at 37, A₅ = 2²³ − 1), and the 3-adic valuation of the net
exponent per direction, added to rule 90's own confinement (3), gives the power. So for 1968276 at
the character 2c, λ = t_j⁴ t_k⁴ λ_V(2c) = t_j³ t_k³ · (element of F(2⁹)), a ninth power; for 1431278,
λ(2c) = t_j^{2²³−2} t_k^{2²³−2} · (element of F(2⁹)) and 3 | 2²³ − 2. The claim is q = 9 · (2¹⁸ + 1)
in F(2³⁶)*, not 27, as the census has it.

**The mechanism and its proof** (proposal.md, section 3): Theorem A, f = (line units) · h^Q

## 4850: the precise negative

4850 = F(a, b), F = s³ + P²s² + P³ + P² + P (s = a + b, P = ab), irreducible; rotated by 45° it is
F' = (ab)³ + (a + b)⁴(1 + a)²(1 + b)² + (a + b)², a cube plus a square. At 37 it is not:

* (units) × (F(2⁹)-valued rule): no solution of the exponent linear system (t₁ generates all
  cubes, so this is linear algebra mod 171) for symmetric orbit products of radius ≤ 4 with any
  exponents, for any three directions of (Z/37)² with any exponents, nor modulo 27th or 19th roots
  of unity (`scratch/orbsearch*.py`, `scratch/alldirs.py`, `scratch/linsearch.py`);
* g³ · U · (F(2⁹)-valued) for any g of radius ≤ 6 and seven units U (`scratch/cubesearch.py`);
* a twisted pair or sparse power (at 37 every dilation is a Frobenius power; the lightest of the
  3¹¹ symmetric cube roots has 440 of 1369 terms, chance level; `scratch/cuberoots.py`);
* certified line by line (lines k ≥ 4 have irreducible factors of degree 13 to 35; the first
  consultation's cyclotomic certificates are vacuous since 1 + ζ has order 37 · 87381 and
  ⟨1 + ζ⟩ ∩ F(2¹⁸)* is exactly the cubes; `scratch/linefactor.py`, `scratch/zeta19.py`);
* order-9 Gauss sums times ninth powers (those Gauss sums are 27th roots of unity; 4850's
  eigenvalues have a nontrivial μ₁₉-part, ninth powers being F(2⁹)* · μ₃ · μ₁₉; `scratch/gauss.py`).

It is not confined at 109 or 185 either, the other sizes with s = 18 (`scratch/othersizes.log`).

## What I tried and what failed

linear algebra; it found the four radius-5 targets' structure at once (their class logs equal rule
7's on every class) and produced the exclusions above. The radius-6 census gave the population for
reach. What failed: every multiplicative decomposition of 4850 I could formulate; the
cube-plus-square form, F' = N_{F(2⁵⁴)/F(2¹⁸)}(π + c) with c³ = Y², which moves the question to
F(2⁵⁴) without a reason; the 19-cyclotomic model of F(2¹⁸) (t₂ = ζ₁₉⁵ + ζ₁₉⁹ + ζ₁₉¹⁵ + ζ₁₉¹⁶ + ζ₁₉¹⁷),
suggestive but unused. The regression against the three previous pilots' conditions on the atlas
did not finish.
