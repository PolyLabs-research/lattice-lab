Stage 4 is still running (tori up to n = 400 are being added). "Group" is Zl×Zm written as Zg×Zlm/g, g = gcd(l, m): tori with the same group carry the same codes. Laws: stage 3b's registered cap 1.40√n (failed), stage 4's registered envelope 0.801·n0.607 (failed at n = 306), and on the score view the published [[288,12,18]]'s 13.5 and stage 4's registered ceiling 17. The published [[360,12,24]] (Bravyi et al.; d = 24 by this project's registered study) is in the same family and scores 19.2.

Fits (exploratory; examples/qec-census-4/regularity): the top edge is a k = 4 staircase on n divisible by 6, described by 0.617·√(n ln n) rounded down to even. Over n ≤ 400 it is indistinguishable from about 0.78·n0.613, and from n ≳ 90 from 1.75·√n − 4.6 (where d/√n levels off). Below the edge, each k layer follows about 2.31·n0.443 − k (within one even step for 92–94% of stage-4 points, fitted on n ≤ 300). Choose a single k to see its layer.
